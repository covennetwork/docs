Coven routes trades across Uniswap v3 and v4 pools on [Arc](/arc/), Circle's chain where gas is paid in USDC. It finds the best route across every pool for a pair, shows new pools within seconds of their creation, and moves USDC to and from ten other chains through Circle's CCTP.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="One line to quote" icon="rocket">
    `createCoven().quote({ tokenIn, tokenOut, amountIn })` prices a trade across v3 and v4, after fees.
  </Card>
  <Card title="New pairs, live" icon="star">
    Watch pools as they are created, with lookalike tokens flagged.
  </Card>
  <Card title="USDC in and out" icon="random">
    Bridge with Circle's Forwarding Service, including gas paid in USDC on the source chain.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Built on viem" icon="seti:typescript">
    Works with every wagmi connector, in browsers and in Node.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
